# Sources for the Japanese course

Each unit of the Japanese track is anchored to one classic of modern Japanese literature. Every
level quotes a short line from that work, and the reading level at the end of each unit gives a
longer passage. Quotes are copied verbatim from the Aozora Bunko (青空文庫) e-texts listed below,
with the ruby readings (《…》) and editorial notes (［＃…］) stripped. Chapter labels (一, 上 先生と私 一,
九、ジョバンニの切符, …) are the section headings of those texts. Stories with no divisions are
labelled “(single-part story)”. Vocabulary, practice sentences and blanks were written for this
course.

The units run from the simplest prose (children’s stories written mostly in kana) to Sōseki’s
novels.

## Public-domain status

All five authors died more than 70 years ago (the latest, Dazai Osamu, in 1948), so their works are
in the public domain in Japan (life + 50 years for authors who died before 1968; the 2018 extension
to 70 years was not retroactive) and in countries that use life + 70 years. Aozora Bunko publishes
only public-domain texts. Our quotes come from Aozora’s plain-text transcriptions of these works, not
from the modern editors’ notes or commentary in the base editions.

## Notes on the texts

- In 蜘蛛の糸 the robber’s name 犍陀多 (Kandata) uses a character outside Shift_JIS. Aozora marks
  it as ※［＃「特のへん＋廴＋聿」、第3水準1-87-71］. Our cleaned copy restores it as 犍.
- Readings (romaji and kana) follow the Aozora ruby where it exists (e.g. こころ reads 私 as
  わたくし, 蜘蛛の糸 reads 己 as おれ). Elsewhere they follow standard modern readings.
- Where a quote leaves words out, the gap is marked with “…”.
